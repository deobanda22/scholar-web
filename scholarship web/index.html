Different starting points

A route for your next qualification.

Whether you have MSCE, a diploma or a degree—or want a shorter certificate course—explore the entry requirements and funding for the specific programme you choose.

01

MSCE holders

Explore undergraduate degrees, foundation programmes and eligible certificate courses. Entry requirements vary by university and subject.

02

Diploma holders

Look into further diplomas or degree programmes. Ask institutions whether prior learning or credit transfer is accepted.

03

Degree holders

Compare postgraduate certificates, master's programmes and research degrees, including their separate funding criteria.

04

Short courses

Find short courses and professional certificates. Scholarships may not cover short courses, so confirm fees and funding first.

Funding depends on the award. Some scholarships cover full costs; others cover only part of tuition or living expenses. Many courses have no scholarship funding. Check the official award terms and university entry rules before making plans or payments.
